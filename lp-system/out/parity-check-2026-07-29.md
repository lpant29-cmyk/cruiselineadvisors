## Summary

1. The official page's skeleton is fully SSR-verified by curl (hero, pricing bar, 7 day-chapters with language/currency/travel-tips, ship section, similar-cruises shell); only live values (calendar prices, arrive/depart times, reviews) hydrate client-side, and their pricing data matches our cached cruiseSearch GraphQL (4 stateroom classes, per-sailing dates, taxesAndFeesIncluded=true).
2. Core product parity holds: hero identity, day-by-day with per-port blurbs and currency/language, sea days from ship highlights, ship module, taxes disclosure, travel-docs flag, closing CTA.
3. Theirs-only, all transactional or account features we structurally cannot have: per-date price calendar, occupancy and stateroom pickers, Book now, excursion drawers, reviews, save/share, route map image (the one gap worth closing with a licensed asset).
4. Ours-richer: dated and stamped fare provenance, deposits guidance, on-page FAQ with calling-cost first, why-call trio, and the required non-affiliation disclosure and referral legal footer.
5. Intentional divergences are all funnel-model driven (call instead of checkout, monthly lead-ins instead of a stale date grid, no nav or cross-sell, noindex) and match the ITINERARY DETAIL LP ruling baked into the template header.
