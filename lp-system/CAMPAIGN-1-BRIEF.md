## Two rules that govern everything below

**1. No trademarks in ad text.** Cruise line and ship names may appear in
KEYWORDS. They must never appear in headlines, descriptions, sitelink text,
sitelink descriptions or callouts. We are an independent referral service,
not an authorised reseller. "Galveston", "Texas", "cruise" are all fine.

**2. Every ad group's keyword is mirrored in the page heading.** One ad
group = one keyword theme = one URL parameter = one matching H1. No keyword
sits in a group whose landing page cannot show its exact match.

## Note on passing the raw keyword in the URL

Google's `{keyword}` ValueTrack would let the page echo the search term
directly. We use a fixed whitelist instead: the page only accepts known
codes and ignores anything else. Echoing raw query text into a headline is
an injection risk and can produce nonsense or misleading headings, and a
heading claiming a ship or length the page cannot show is exactly the
message-match failure this design exists to prevent. Adding a new variant
takes one line, so the whitelist costs nothing and stays safe.
