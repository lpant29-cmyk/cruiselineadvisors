### 1. HIGH — ?kw= can make p002 claim to be Royal Caribbean's official site

p002 rewrites its H1 from the `?kw=` query parameter. The BAD_KW denylist blocks
claim words but not affiliation claims. All of these are accepted today on the
LIVE page at site/en/go/lines/royal-caribbean/from/galveston/:

- `?kw=royal+caribbean+official+site`      -> H1 "Royal Caribbean Official Site"
- `?kw=royal+caribbean+authorized+agent`   -> H1 "Royal Caribbean Authorized Agent"
- `?kw=royal+caribbean+reservations`       -> H1 "Royal Caribbean Reservations"
- `?kw=carnival+cruises+from+galveston`    -> H1 names a line with zero inventory

Violates Hard Rule 2 (never imply affiliation) and Hard Rule 5 (never fabricate
credentials), on brand keywords, so it is also trademark exposure.

Plus a demonstrable guard bypass from statement ordering: sanitising runs BEFORE
BAD_KW, and it replaces "%" and "$" with a space. `?kw=50%+off+royal+caribbean`
matches BAD_KW as raw input but is sanitised to "50 off royal caribbean" first,
so the `% ?off` alternative never sees a "%" and the keyword is ACCEPTED as an H1.
Separately the regex's leading `\b` applies to the whole alternation, which makes
the `\$` and `% ?off` alternatives nearly unmatchable even on raw input.

Fix: run BAD_KW on the RAW parameter before character stripping; add affiliation
terms (official, authorised/authorized, direct, agent, reservations, customer
service) and the other cruise line names to the denylist. Needs page-builder.
