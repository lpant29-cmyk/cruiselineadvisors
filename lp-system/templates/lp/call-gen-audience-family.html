    Family cruising — quick answers

    Are kids clubs really free?

    On major family lines, daytime and evening clubs for roughly ages 3–17 are included. Nurseries and late-night sitting usually cost extra per hour.

    Do kids-sail-free deals have a catch?

    They apply to specific dates and cabin types, and kids still pay taxes and fees. Sometimes another promo beats it — that's the math we run for you.

    Can all four of us share one cabin?

    Yes — quad cabins with pull-down berths are standard, but they sell out first for school breaks. Book the cabin type early; the call takes care of it.

    Is calling more expensive than booking online?

    No. Same fares, plus a specialist stacking family promos and picking cabins that actually work — free.
