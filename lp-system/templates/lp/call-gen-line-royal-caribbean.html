    Booking Royal Caribbean — quick answers

    Is calling more expensive than booking on the cruise line's site?

    No — the fare is the cruise line's fare either way. The call adds comparison, promo-stacking and a human who handles changes afterward.

    Will I keep my loyalty benefits?

    Yes. Your loyalty number attaches to the booking exactly as if you booked direct.

    Which ship is best for families?

    Usually the biggest and newest — but the honest answer depends on your kids' ages and your budget. That's a two-minute conversation.

    Can you help if the price drops after booking?

    Before final payment, usually yes — repricing, upgrades or credit. The partner agency that books you handles it.
